5. DECISION OQ-004 — 70% VALIDATOR FEE DISTRIBUTION
============================================================

LOCKED ECONOMIC RULE:

70% of applicable gas/fee economics → validators.

20% → PrimeWave Treasury.

10% → burn.

The 70% validator allocation must NOT be confused with the 15M staking reward reserve.

They are two separate mechanisms.

Architecture:

Gas/fees
    │
    ├── 70% → Validator Fee Vault
    │
    ├── 20% → PrimeWave Treasury
    │
    └── 10% → Burn

Validator Fee Vault:

Accumulates ongoing transaction-fee revenue.

The validator fee pool should be distributed according to validator eligibility/weight rather than simply giving the entire amount to the block producer.

The recommended model is:

validator fee entitlement ∝ effective validator weight × eligibility/uptime

Delegators should receive the appropriate portion according to the finalized staking/delegation model.

For the initial 15 validators, if all have equal weight and equal eligibility, their entitlement should be approximately equal.

The exact implementation must be documented before coding.

IMPORTANT:

Do not mix:

A. Validator Fee Vault
B. 15M PW RewardVault

They must remain separate.

============================================================

LOCKED DECISION:

PWUSD has ONE designated Monetary Authority.

Emergency security:
