============================================================
7. VALIDATOR SYNCHRONIZATION KEEPER
============================================================

This is the most important C09 production gap.

Current architecture:

P-Chain
→ Warp
→ ICM Relayer
→ PoAManager
→ PrimeWaveValidatorRegistry
→ ValidatorFeeVault

The missing operational trigger is:

PrimeWaveValidatorRegistry.syncValidator(validationId)

Implement a lightweight production-oriented keeper service.

The keeper must:

- observe authoritative validator state/events
- identify validators requiring synchronization
- call permissionless syncValidator()
- retry failed synchronization
- avoid duplicate harmful operations
- maintain state
- expose health status
- emit operational logs
- alert on persistent failure

CRITICAL:

The keeper is NOT a validator authority.

The keeper MUST NOT:

- create validators
- invent validator IDs
- assign arbitrary weight
- deactivate arbitrary validators
- change validator ownership
- alter RewardVault
- alter FeeDistributor
- alter governance
- mint PW

The smart contract remains the authority.

The keeper merely triggers permissionless synchronization.
