16. CLAIM MODEL
============================================================

Prefer a pull-based claim model.

Validator/recipient:

claim()

rather than:

- lower gas
- recipient controls claim timing

2. update accounting checkpoint
3. transfer native PW
4. emit event
5. prevent double claim
